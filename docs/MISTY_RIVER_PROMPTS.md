The user, 2026-10-23: "faz mais uma intermediária entre a 1 e 2, acertar a ordem das outras". The draft that had fallen out of the

The draft's story: "Um rio subterrâneo divide terraços de rocha. Duas pontes naturais cruzam o leito; as galerias do sul conduzem à
Colônia do Micélio Profundo" (Voss: "O rio corre para dentro da montanha"). The level's own look, unlike the others (basalt and ember;
roots and amber; mushrooms and cyan; the silver crown and gold): a river of black water between terraces of wet black shale, bone-white
gypsum crystals grown into cave flowers, veins of fluorite glowing violet, silver mist over the water, and the warm white lamps the old
river-wardens left on their mooring posts.

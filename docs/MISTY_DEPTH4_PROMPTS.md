# Misty Cave, map 4: O Underdark de Mordávia (mordavia-underdark): the art

The user, 2026-10-23: "escreve os prompts do mapa 4, o Underdark de Mordávia, com visual próprio; a saída dele abre o Atlas do
Submundo"; earlier: the Misty Cave is one of the two ways into the Underworld and "will lead to the top of the RootCrown Enclave".
The chain: level 1 the Misty Cave (basalt, calcite, ember), level 2 the Garganta das Raízes (petrified roots, slate, amber sap), level 3
the Bacia dos Esporos (ivory mushrooms, cyan glow), map 4 here, whose exit opens the Atlas do Submundo de Mordávia.

The map's own look, the great reveal of scale: the party climbs out onto the crown of the Root-Crown, a colossal living tree whose
roots they have followed down, on the shore of the inner sea. Silver-white bark, the old Enclave's carved platforms with warm gold
lanterns, the Glasswater Sea far below in pale aqua glass, the cavern's roof studded with countless glowing crystal points that read
as another night sky (the draft's briefing: "um mar interior, ilhas de ruínas e bosques de fungos sob um teto que parece outro céu";
Voss: "Mordávia tem outro horizonte debaixo do próprio chão"), and far off the ember glow of the God-King's Deep Port. That "sky" is
the panorama's painting only: the game's one sky is never drawn underground.
